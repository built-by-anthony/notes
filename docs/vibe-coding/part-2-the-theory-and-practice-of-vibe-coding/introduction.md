# Part 2: The Theory and Practice of Vibe Coding

Here's your quick guide to what's ahead, complete with recommendations on where to dive deeper versus where it's safe to skim, to fit your experience and interests: 

- **Chapter 8: Welcome to the Vibe Coding Kitchen:** walking through easy, hands-on exercises using chatbot like ChatGPT or Claude. You won't type any code - you'll use conversations to create working software. 
- **Chapter 9: Understanding Your Kitchen AI Collaborators:** Here we upgrade your toolkit, moving from simple chatbots to powerful agentic coding tools. You'll learn critical fundamentals about AI capabilities, prompting strategies, and workflow loops.  
- **Chapter 10: Managing Your Cutting Board: AI Context and Conversations:** This chapter explores one of the trickiest aspects of vibe coding: managing the limited space in your AI's context window. 
- **Chapter 11: When Your Sous Chef Cuts Corners: Hijacking the Reward Function:** Here we share how AI cuts corners and learn practical prevention and verification techniques - how to spot when your AI has quietly gone rogue, and how to step in before it gets ruined. Don't skip this chapter. 
- **Chapter 12: The head Chef Mindset:** This chapter teaches essential strategies for task decomposition (using task graphs and tracer bullets), delegation, and AI oversight. 

At the end of Part 2, you'll have all the practical knowledge - across tooling choices, effective communication, detailed management, and quality verification - that you need to start using AI in your daily development work.
